Problem statement

Why current approaches fail

Most assessments track only single factors or publish static annual reports that miss rapid shocks.

  • Single-factor analysis hides poor household vulnerability.
  • Siloed planning ignores regional trade cascades.
  • Aid deployed after crises peak, not early warnings.

Project objective

AI-powered framework with multi-factor FSI, vulnerability diagnosis, and targeted interventions.

Multi-factor FSI (0–100) Vulnerability diagnosis Reserve optimization Systemic risk mapping
AI assessment framework

Data & indicators

Integrates production, trade, storage, prices, and health outcomes into unified model.

  • Per-country production (Pᵢ), requirements (Cᵢ), storage (S).
  • Import networks with reliability scores.
  • Health outcomes for FSI validation.

AI engines

ML models simulate shocks and recommend preventive interventions.
